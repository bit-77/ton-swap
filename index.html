FAQ

Everything About
TON-Swap

All your questions about swapping and staking on TON-Swap, answered comprehensively.

What is TON-Swap?+
TON-Swap is a TRON-native DEX aggregator and staking platform built around the TON token on the TRON blockchain. It finds the best TRC20 token swap routes across all major TRON liquidity pools — including SunSwap, JustSwap, SunStable, and more — executing everything in a single non-custodial transaction. TON-Swap also features a full staking module where users earn up to 38% APY in daily TON rewards.
What is the TON token on TRON?+
TON is the native utility and governance token of the TON-Swap ecosystem, deployed on TRON as a TRC20 token. Holders benefit from reduced swap fees, staking rewards distributed daily, and governance voting rights over future protocol upgrades. TON is tradeable on TON-Swap and all major TRON-based DEX platforms.
How do I swap TRC20 tokens on TON-Swap?+
Connect your TronLink wallet using the "Connect Wallet" button in the top navigation. Select your source TRC20 token (e.g., USDT) and your destination token (e.g., TON). Review the quoted exchange rate, estimated output, route breakdown, and fees. Click "Swap Now" and approve the transaction in your TronLink wallet. Your swapped tokens arrive in under 3 seconds thanks to TRON's ultra-fast block finality.
How do I stake TON tokens and earn rewards?+
Navigate to the Staking section on TON-Swap. Connect your TronLink wallet, enter the amount of TON you wish to stake, choose a lock period (7 days at 7% APY up to 365 days at 38% APY), review the reward preview and your projected unlock date, then click "Stake TON". Rewards are calculated per block and distributed daily directly to your connected wallet as claimable TON tokens.
What is the maximum APY on TON-Swap staking?+
The maximum staking APY on TON-Swap is 38%, available through the 365-day TON Diamond pool. Shorter commitment periods offer: 7% APY for the 7-day Flex pool, 16% APY for the 30-day Silver pool, and 28% APY for the 90-day Gold pool. All rewards are denominated in TON tokens and distributed daily. The longer your commitment, the greater your yield.
What fees does TON-Swap charge for swaps?+
TON-Swap charges a protocol fee of 0.05% on each swap — one of the lowest rates among all TRON DEX platforms. TON token holders receive an additional 20% discount, reducing their effective fee to just 0.04%. TRON network bandwidth costs are negligible, typically under $0.001 per transaction. There are no hidden charges; the rate shown always includes all applicable fees.
Is TON-Swap safe and have the smart contracts been audited?+
Yes. TON-Swap is fully non-custodial — your funds never leave your wallet until a swap settles atomically on TRON. All smart contracts have been independently audited by reputable blockchain security firms. MEV protection is built into every transaction to shield you from front-running bots. If any swap cannot complete for any reason, all tokens are automatically returned to your wallet with no loss.
Which wallets are supported on TON-Swap?+
TON-Swap is fully optimized for TronLink (both browser extension and mobile app), which is the recommended wallet for the best TRON DeFi experience. It also supports TokenPocket, Trust Wallet (on TRON network), WalletConnect-compatible TRON wallets, and imToken. No account registration or email is ever required — simply connect your wallet and begin swapping or staking immediately.
Does TON-Swap require KYC or identity verification?+
Absolutely not. TON-Swap is a fully decentralized protocol with zero account registration, no email requirements, no identity verification (KYC), and no personal data collection of any kind. Your wallet address is your entire identity on-chain. All you need is a compatible TRON wallet such as TronLink to access the full platform.
How does TON-Swap find the best swap rate?+
TON-Swap's routing algorithm simultaneously queries over 120 TRON liquidity pools, including SunSwap V1 & V2, JustSwap, SunStable, and all other active TRON DEX protocols in real time. An advanced pathfinding algorithm then optimally splits your order across multiple pools to minimize price impact and maximize your output amount. Quotes refresh automatically every 5 seconds so you always act on accurate, live pricing.
Can I unstake my TON tokens before the lock period ends?+
Early unstaking is available, but it incurs a penalty fee designed to protect long-term stakers. Penalties range from 5% of staked principal for the 7-day pool up to 15% for the 365-day Diamond pool. Any rewards accrued up to the early exit point remain fully claimable without penalty. The Flex pool (no lock-up) can be exited at any time with zero penalty.
What is slippage tolerance and how should I configure it?+
Slippage tolerance defines the acceptable price deviation between your quoted rate and the final executed rate, caused by market movement during transaction confirmation. For stablecoin swaps (e.g., USDT to USDD), 0.1% is sufficient. For TON or other volatile TRC20 tokens, 0.5% is recommended. For large orders during high-volatility periods, 1.0% provides better execution success. The "Auto" setting uses TON-Swap's real-time volatility model to select the optimal tolerance automatically.
What is MEV protection and why does TON-Swap use it?+
MEV (Maximal Extractable Value) bots scan public blockchain mempools and front-run swap transactions, forcing you to pay inflated prices and extracting value from your trade without your consent. TON-Swap routes all transactions through a private relay layer on TRON, making them invisible to MEV bots before confirmation. This protection saves users significant value on larger trades, with absolutely no extra steps required from you.
Does TON-Swap support cross-chain swaps?+
Yes. While TON-Swap is primarily optimized for TRON-native TRC20 swaps, it also supports cross-chain routes to and from Ethereum, BNB Chain, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana, and additional networks via integrated bridge protocols. A cross-chain swap automatically handles the source-chain swap, the bridge transfer, and the destination-chain swap in a single user action — though cross-chain transactions take slightly longer than native TRON swaps.
How are TON staking rewards calculated and distributed?+
TON staking rewards are calculated on a per-block basis using the annualized APY rate for your chosen pool tier. Every 24 hours, the protocol takes a snapshot and distributes earned TON rewards proportionally to all active stakers. Rewards accumulate in a dedicated claimable balance shown in your staking dashboard, which you can withdraw to your wallet at any time without touching your staked principal.
Can I get swap quotes without connecting a wallet?+
Yes. You can enter any token pair and input amount on TON-Swap to view live rate quotes, routing breakdowns, price impact estimates, and full fee previews without ever connecting a wallet. Wallet connection is only required when you are ready to execute the swap or stake your tokens. This lets you freely compare rates and plan trades before committing any funds.
What TRC20 tokens can I swap on TON-Swap?+
TON-Swap supports all TRC20 tokens that have active liquidity on TRON, including TON, USDT (TRC20), TRX, USDD, SUN, JST, WIN, BTT, USDJ, and hundreds more. Any TRC20 token listed on a TRON DEX is automatically discoverable on TON-Swap. If you have a contract address for a token not in the default list, you can paste it directly into the token search field to add it instantly.
Is there a TON-Swap mobile app?+
TON-Swap runs as a fully responsive web application at tonswap-staking.vercel.app and works seamlessly inside TronLink's built-in dApp browser on both iOS and Android. You can also access it via TokenPocket's dApp browser on mobile. A dedicated native mobile app is currently in development and will be announced across our official community channels when ready.
How does TON-Swap compare to SunSwap?+
TON-Swap is a DEX aggregator that routes through SunSwap, JustSwap, and every other TRON DEX simultaneously, meaning you get the best rate from all liquidity sources in one place. Trading directly on SunSwap limits you to a single set of pools. TON-Swap additionally provides MEV protection, multi-hop and split routing, cross-chain bridging, and a full TON staking module with up to 38% APY — none of which SunSwap offers natively. Users consistently receive a better or equal rate by using TON-Swap.

Community support and real-time assistance are available through our Telegram group, Discord server, and Twitter/X page (links available in the footer). For technical issues or smart contract bug reports, use the GitHub repository or submit through the Bug Bounty program. Response times for urgent swap issues on Telegram are typically under 4 hours. Important: TON-Swap team members will never initiate a DM with you first, and will never ask for your private keys or seed phrase under any circumstances.
